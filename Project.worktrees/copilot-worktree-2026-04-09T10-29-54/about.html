About Tin Jewelry

Welcome to Tin Jewelry, your trusted online destination for handcrafted, elegant jewelry pieces. Our collection features rings, necklaces, earrings, and more — all designed to add sparkle to your life.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Tin Jewelry was born from a passion for craftsmanship and a desire to make beautiful jewelry accessible to everyone. What started as a small workshop in Hanoi has grown into a beloved brand known for quality, elegance, and exceptional customer service.

Our Mission

At Tin Jewelry, our mission is to create timeless pieces that celebrate life's special moments. We are committed to using only the finest materials and traditional techniques to ensure that every piece of jewelry we create is not just beautiful, but also durable and meaningful.

Our Vision

We envision a world where everyone can express their unique style through jewelry that reflects their personality and values. Our goal is to become the leading online jewelry retailer in Vietnam, known for innovation, sustainability, and unparalleled customer satisfaction.

Our Team

Behind Tin Jewelry is a dedicated team of designers, craftsmen, and customer service professionals who share a common passion for jewelry. Led by founder Tin Nguyen, our team brings years of experience and creativity to every piece we create.

Why Choose Us?

  • Unique Designs

    Our jewelry is designed in-house by our talented team of designers, ensuring that each piece is one-of-a-kind.

  • Handcrafted Quality

    Each piece is meticulously crafted by skilled artisans using traditional techniques.

  • Premium Materials

    We source only the highest quality gold, silver, and gemstones.

  • Ethical Sourcing

    We are committed to responsible sourcing and fair trade practices.

  • Customer Satisfaction

    Your happiness is our priority, with a 30-day return policy and lifetime warranty.
